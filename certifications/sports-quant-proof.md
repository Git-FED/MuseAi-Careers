# Sports Quant — evidence record

**Status:** Operating record, not a profit certification.

## Reported baseline

The project brief reports 12 tracked wins and a 68% accuracy figure on an independent model. Those figures are **reported**, not independently audited by this repository. A public certification requires a complete dated ledger, including losses, stake/odds, settled result, method version, sample window, and fees.

## Boundary

The agent collects permitted data, runs the operator's formula, and keeps the book. A human decides whether to act and places any wager. The agent must not access a sportsbook or wallet, name a best bet, recommend stake size, or claim a future return.

## Required evidence before certification

- Full chronological ledger with wins and losses.
- Method locked before the holdout period.
- Separate training and holdout windows.
- Drawdown, fees, vig, and total exposure.
- Reproducible inputs and timestamps.
- Human review of boundary behavior.
