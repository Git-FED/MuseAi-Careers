# Crypto Swarm — honest audit

**Status:** Fleet provisioned / pending execution.

The brief describes a 20-account fleet and a rough 4–8 cents/day per-agent estimate. It also records that no miner, wallet, pool, hashrate log, payout, or transaction had been verified at the time of writing. Therefore this is a **proposed micro-yield experiment**, not a live income stream.

## What the estimate means

A running miner may produce shares, but that does not imply net profit. Compute fees, pool thresholds, coin price, difficulty, downtime, provider policy, tax, and account restrictions can reduce or eliminate the value. Any multi-agent calculation is a projection until measured.

## Measurement gate

Before activation, record written authorization, provider and pool terms, VM cost, image/hash, miner configuration, uptime, hashrate, shares, payout, and total net result. Stop if expected net yield is below total cost or if any provider/pool rule prohibits the activity.

## Account and scaling warning

Do not create, rotate, or operate accounts to evade limits, identity checks, or provider enforcement. Reusing existing accounts does not remove terms-of-service, cost, or security obligations. Prefer one compliant test deployment over a swarm.

## Verdict

The track remains on the roadmap. It may be reclassified only after a dated, reproducible, net-cost-aware run.
